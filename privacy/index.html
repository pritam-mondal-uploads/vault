Privacy Policy

Last updated: 02 July 2027

Vault Finance ("we," "our," "the site") provides free financial and tax calculators, articles, and reference tools for Indian personal finance. This page explains what data is collected when you use this site and how it's used.

Information We Collect

Vault Finance's calculators run entirely in your browser. Numbers you enter into any calculator (income, investments, deductions, etc.) are processed locally on your device and are not transmitted to or stored on our servers unless explicitly stated on that tool.

We may collect the following automatically through standard web technologies:

  • Pages visited, time spent, and general usage patterns (via analytics tools, if enabled)
  • Browser type, device type, and approximate location (via IP address)
  • Referring website or search engine

Cookies and Advertising

This site may display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendor cookies by visiting the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out page.

Analytics

We may use analytics services (such as Google Analytics) to understand how visitors use this site, in aggregate and anonymized form. This data helps us improve content and tools; it is not used to personally identify you.

Third-Party Links

Some articles and calculators may link to external resources (government tax portals, official forms, financial institutions). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party sites.

Data We Do Not Collect

We do not require account creation, and we do not ask for or store PAN numbers, bank details, or other sensitive financial identifiers. Any figures entered into calculators stay in your browser session.

Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
