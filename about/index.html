About the project

About Vault Finance

Vault Finance is a free, ad-supported toolkit built to make Indian personal finance and tax calculations simple, accurate, and accessible - without needing a CA, a login, or a spreadsheet.

Why Vault Finance Exists

Indian tax and finance rules change often, and most calculators online are either outdated, buried in ads, or built for a narrow use case. Vault Finance was built to fix that: a single place with tools that stay current, explainers that go deeper than a one-line answer, and an interface that doesn't get in your way.

What's Inside

  • Calculators - Income Tax, Old vs New Regime comparison, In-Hand Salary, HRA, Capital Gains, 80C Planner, NPS, Gratuity, EPF, RSU, and more.
  • Reads - In-depth explainers on how each rule works, written to answer the "why," not just the "what."
  • FAQs - Quick answers to the questions that come up most.
  • ITR Form Selector - A guided tool to help identify which ITR form applies to your situation.

Who Built This

Vault Finance is built and maintained by Pritam Mondal, trying to make finance simple.

A Note on Accuracy

Every calculator and article on Vault Finance is built with care and checked against official sources such as Income Tax Department guidelines, SEBI and AMFI rules, and current regime provisions. That said, tax and finance rules can change, and individual circumstances vary. Vault Finance is a reference and planning tool - it is not a substitute for advice from a qualified Chartered Accountant or financial advisor, especially for high-value or non-standard filings.
